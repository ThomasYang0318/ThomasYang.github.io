OpenGL 3D Rendering System

C++OpenGLOBJShaderLighting

A custom 3D rendering system implemented in C++ to understand the full pipeline behind common 3D software — from model parsing and mesh construction to shaders, materials, textures, and lighting.

Rendering pipeline

The project begins by reading OBJ files and parsing vertices, faces, and material information. The renderer then builds meshes and uses shaders to calculate lighting and material effects before drawing the 3D model to the screen.

Lighting demo ↗OBJ loader ↗

Lighting & texturing

The renderer supports material processing, texturing, and multiple light types, making the differences between directional, spot, point, and combined lighting visible in the final output.
